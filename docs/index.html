    About Bookmarklets:
    • Fragility: They rely on Blackboard's structure and may break after updates.
    • Risk: Execute JavaScript directly; only use trusted sources.
    • Flexibility: Drag links to your bookmarks bar and use on Blackboard pages.
    To install: Drag a bookmarklet button to your browser's bookmarks bar. If dragging doesn't work, right-click the button and choose Bookmark This Link, or click Copy to manually create a bookmark with the code as the URL.
    Disclaimer: These bookmarklets are experimental and provided as-is, without warranty or official support from Blackboard or Anthology. Use at your own risk, and only in non-production or test environments until thoroughly verified.
